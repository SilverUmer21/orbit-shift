# Home screen plan: round 3

## What was wrong with round 2
- It was a generic mobile layout (top chips, a card, a wide Play bar, a dock) wearing our colours. Nothing about it could only be Orbit Shift.
- The plates were too big. Play was a full-width bar and the dock tabs were 62px tall.

## Hard rules for this round
- **Size budget.** Play is at most 150x48 (or a 64px ring). Secondary actions are at most 40px visually, with a 48px invisible hit area. No full-width bars on Home.
- **The world stays calm.** The plane orbiting the planet, the mission status and the UI are the heroes. No scenery competing with them.
- **The UI speaks the game's language.** Orbit, gap, gate, fold, constellation. If a control could appear in any other mobile game, it is not done.
- **Mission status stays.** It is the part you liked from A, but it must be compact and tappable (opens the missions sheet).
- **Thumb reach.** Frequent actions live in the bottom half or on the screen edges.
- **Competitive later.** Keep a Friends slot, quietly marked soon.

## Four concepts
| | Idea | Why it is ours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H Constellation Chart | The menu is constellations in the sky. Garage is drawn as the plane, Settings as a gear, Daily as a flame, and missions are the brightest stars. Play is the planet's gate ring. | Controls are not buttons; they are star maps that light up. |
| J Bookmark Tabs | Small paper index tabs poke in from the screen edges. Missions pull out as a page from the side. Play is a small gold flap on the bottom edge. | Paper craft, tiny footprint, and the centre stays free. |
| M Gate Dock | Every button is a tiny gate ring with a gap (the game's core shape) and turns its gap on press. Missions are activity rings around the planet. | The core mechanic becomes the UI vocabulary. |
| P Instant Orbit | No Play button. The world is live and tapping anywhere starts. Tiny icons sit in a row at the bottom. Missions hug the planet as arcs. | The fastest path into the game, which suits hyper-casual. |

## Process
1. Build all four with real art, then review each one for size, creativity and clarity.
2. Iterate on screenshots until I would be happy to show them.
3. You pick, or combine, and I tune.
